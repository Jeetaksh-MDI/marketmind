PRIVACY

Small-data by design.

Short version:

MarketMind asks for a display name and records simulation choices so it can generate your Economic Decision Profile and count game usage. It does not need your email, phone number, bank details, precise location or financial accounts.

What may be stored

If server-side tracking is enabled by the site owner: your display name, anonymous session ID, game start/completion timestamps, round progress, your simulation decisions, derived trait scores, resulting personality type, final fictional game state, browser user-agent and referring page.

Why it is used

To generate and troubleshoot the simulation, understand completion rates, count players and improve the game. The behavioral result is an educational game output, not a clinical, financial, employment or psychological assessment.

Local storage

Your browser stores your current game state so refreshing the page does not erase your progress. Restarting the game clears that local simulation state.

What not to enter

Use a first name, nickname or non-sensitive display name. Do not enter passwords, account numbers, government identifiers, payment credentials or confidential information.

Data removal

Before public launch, replace this paragraph with the operator's contact email or deletion-request workflow. The included Supabase schema makes individual sessions removable by session ID or name through the database dashboard.

Decision-profile disclaimer

MarketMind infers scenario-based tendencies from a short simulation. Scores can vary with context and should not be treated as validated psychological traits, investment advice, hiring evidence or diagnosis.
